# Drawing inventory – `D:\Projects\Drawings\ISU Files`

Source folder is treated as **read-only**; nothing from it is copied into this repository.
File-level listing (546 files, page counts, sheet sizes, vector/scan flag) is generated by
`tools/inventory_drawings.py` into [`drawings_files.csv`](drawings_files.csv). This document is the
human-readable summary, written after inspecting title blocks, cover sheets and sample sheets of every set.

Folder also contains `ISU Files.zip` (752 MB, archive of the same folders), six undownloaded iCloud stubs
(`*.icloud`, Blumberg Hall bid drawings + specs, Addenda 1 and 4) and an earlier `isu_data/` run of
`collect_isu_data.py` (ignored; the repo keeps its own run under `data/`).

## Decisions for the model

1. Detailed (drawing-based) buildings: **Hulman Center, Student Recreation Center, Reeve Hall (East + West), Fine Arts Building, Technology Building (cTED, modelled in its 2025 renovated form), Welcome Center, former School of Nursing (African American Cultural Center)**; Myers Technology Center gets drawing-accurate floor outlines with estimated heights.
2. The School of Nursing Phase II building still stands (now the African American Cultural Center) and is modelled from its scanned elevations. Lincoln Quad was demolished in 2021 and is not modelled; its drawings are catalogued only.
3. Holmstedt Hall drawings cover the plaza only; the hall is a mass model.
4. Blumberg Hall has no drawings in the folder (iCloud stubs); Sycamore Towers are mass models.
